# Special services and materials override

- Materials remain one platform-provided toggle; do not expose brands, products, or inventory quantity to the customer.
- A special service supports multiple decimal-quantity items, item dirtiness, notes/images, and selected sessions.
- Present duration and price snapshots before confirmation. Field errors attach to the affected item and never clear other item input.
- Service execution states use text and icons for pending, in progress, completed, and unable-to-complete.
