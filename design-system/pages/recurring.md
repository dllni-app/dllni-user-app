# Recurring override

- Session selection shows date, time, worker coverage, price, and state; selection is never conveyed by color alone.
- A change proposal compares old/new schedule and financial delta before submission.
- Rejection presents three explicit recovery paths: choose an eligible replacement, revert, or cancel. Never auto-replace.
- Explain the 24-hour rules and the no-movement window near service time before the user reaches a disabled action.
